The method

The TRC Method

How every huddle thinks. It puts reacting where it belongs, after the facts and inside a framework, never at random on a blank whiteboard. The result is focused thinking and decisions that actually get done.

  1. Track

    Bring what is true into the room before anyone reacts. Results, and answers to the right questions.

  2. React

    Put a draft in front of the room, inside a proven framework. Push back, sharpen it, make it yours.

  3. Commit

    Decide the next best steps. Every one gets an owner and a date, confirmed out loud.

Then the next huddle tracks what you committed to.
