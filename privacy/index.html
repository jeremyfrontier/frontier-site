Privacy

Your information, plainly.

This site is run by Jeremy Rivers, Frontier Coaching, LLC, in McKinney, Texas. Here is what I collect, why, where it lives, and what I never do with it.

Last updated November 10, 2026.

What I collect

  • When you take the Baseline Snapshot: your name, email, organization (optional), sector, role, team size, and your answers.
  • When you build a Work Review: your name, email, organization, title, organization type, industry, team size, your goal for the year, the metrics you choose, and the spreadsheet you build.
  • When you email me or book a call: whatever you choose to share.

This site uses no tracking cookies, no analytics, and no advertising pixels. Its fonts and files are hosted on the site itself.

Why I collect it

  • To send you your Snapshot results or your Work Review.
  • To follow up with you personally about what your results mean.
  • To see who these tools are serving, so I can make them better.

Building a Work Review also subscribes you to the Uncommon Work newsletter on Substack, as the page says before you start. Substack handles your subscription under its own privacy policy, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Where it lives

Your answers are saved to a private Google Sheet in my Google Workspace account, and your results email comes from my Gmail. Only I can see them. Calls are booked through Google Calendar.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps basic server logs, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

What I never do

  • I never sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • I never share your individual Snapshot answers with anyone.
  • I never add you to a list you didn’t ask to join.

Your choices

Want to see what I have, correct it, or have it deleted? Email jeremy@thefrontiergroup.co and I’ll take care of it within 30 days.

This site is for adults leading organizations. It isn’t meant for anyone under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect their information.

If any of this changes, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.
