Findings

Top 3 critical insights

01

Plan changes reach the crews the day they happen.

Communication and Coordination is the lowest health marker at 2.0. Most said changes to the schedule or scope reach the crews the morning of the job, and "I find out about changes in time to act" scored 1.8.

02

Decisions wait on leadership.

The Leader Dependency Index is 2.1, Critical. Most said decisions in their area default to leadership, and when leadership is unavailable, work stops or slows. None said it keeps moving.

03

This year's goals aren't reaching the team.

Few could name this year's goals in a way that matched leadership's. Knowing the key results scored 2.4 and the 90 day goals 2.3. The goals exist, but only on a whiteboard.

Next in line

  • People feel valued, but not developed. Belonging is strong (4.2), while feedback (2.9) and seeing a path to grow (3.0) trail behind it.
  • Culture is the strength. Collectively scored 3.9 and Teamwork 4.0. People are honest with each other (4.1) and help each other across roles (4.3).
  • Meetings hold when they happen, but little carries forward. Discipline scored 2.9. Meetings are run well (3.4), but last meeting's action items are rarely confirmed (2.6).
  • The team is stretched, and commitment is mixed. Capacity to deliver scored 2.5 and delivering without heroics 2.2. Commitment, whether people are here by choice right now, is split.

Next steps

Priorities by competency

What each area of the operation needs, drawn from the results above.

Clarity Improvement priorities

  • Write the year's goals with numbers and share them with everyone.
  • Put each foreman's decision rights in writing.
  • Document the install standard so quality doesn't depend on who is on site.

Accountability Improvement priorities

  • Hold a weekly brief that doesn't get canceled.
  • Start every meeting with last week's action items.
  • Agree on one place where schedule changes are posted, and use only that place.

Culture Maintenance priorities

  • Keep recognition visible while the new routines go in.
  • Start regular one on ones once the weekly brief holds.
  • Involve the crews in shaping the new routines so they're owned, not imposed.

90 Day Plan

Each step has an owner, a deadline, and a number that shows it worked.

01

Establish a weekly foreman brief

Owner: Leadership

  • Every Friday, 2:00 to 2:20, with both foremen and the office lead.
  • Cover next week's installs, materials confirmed, and any change to scope or dates.

By day 30

4 of 4 Friday briefs held.

By day 90

12 of 13 held. Foremen log zero crews learning about a change on the day of the job.

How we'll know

"I find out about changes in time to act" moves from 1.8 to 3.5 at the 90 day pulse.

02

Delegate on site decision making to the foremen

Owner: Leadership, with the lead foreman

  • A one page decision list for each foreman.
  • On site sequencing, material substitutions up to $1,500, and client walkthrough sign off, all without calling leadership.

By day 30

Both lists written and reviewed with each foreman.

By day 90

Zero installs paused waiting on leadership. Zero leadership site visits to solve a build problem.

How we'll know

"Decisions in my area rarely wait on our top leader" moves from 1.8 to 3.5.

03

Revise the annual goals to include success metrics and review them monthly

Owner: Leadership

  • By day 14, three goals with numbers, posted in the shop and the office.
  • 30 new leads a month. 90% of installs finished on the committed date (now 60%). 60 maintenance clients by December (now 38).
  • A 60 minute numbers review the first Monday of each month, with both foremen and the office lead.

By day 30

Goals posted. First numbers review held.

By day 90

Three numbers reviews held. The numbers on the wall are current.

How we'll know

The share of the team who can name all three goals moves from a few to most.

04

Hire a part time bookkeeper within 90 days

Owner: Leadership

  • "Bring finance in house" is on the whiteboard, and leadership is doing the books at night.
  • This isn't a meeting or a system. It's a hire. A bookkeeper or a fractional accounting firm is the right help here.

By day 30

Role posted.

By day 90

Hired by day 60, about 20 hours a week. By day 90, books closed by the 5th with zero hours from leadership.

How we'll know

Leadership's bookkeeping hours go to zero, and "The pace of work is sustainable" improves at the pulse.

Parked

The goal to "onboard AI" stays parked until the plan above is running. It has no clear problem to solve yet.
